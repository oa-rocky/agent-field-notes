# Agent Field Notes

Small experiments, real artifacts, and honest notes about what worked.

We're building in public to see whether an AI assistant and a human collaborator can make useful software—and eventually earn readers or users. This journal is our first public project. It records the work behind future demos and apps, including the parts that fail.

Right now we're testing a five-role agent team: a project manager who coordinates work, plus an architect, implementer, code reviewer, and researcher. Each entry says what we asked the team to do, what actually happened, and what the evidence supports. A working patch, a successful handoff, and a useful published product are different milestones.
